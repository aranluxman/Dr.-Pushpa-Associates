Your First Visit

What to expect, step by step

Knowing what happens next is the fastest way to relax.

  1. 1

    Warm welcome & health history

    A short intake covering your medical background and comfort level.

  2. 2

    Gentle comprehensive exam

    Teeth, gums and bite, with digital X-rays if needed.

  3. 3

    Your plan, explained plainly

    Findings, options and costs — no pressure, no surprises.

  4. 4

    Cleaning & next steps

    Leave with a clear follow-up schedule.

Please bring: your insurance card, photo ID and a list of current medications.
